External sources

External sources refer to any resources originating outside the knowledge graph itself. Common examples include websites, documents, databases, large language models (see verbalization-based approaches), and other knowledge graphs (see ontology-based techniques and embedding-based techniques).

The successful use of external sources for knowledge cleaning depends on two key factors: source quality and the alignment process. Source quality refers to attributes such as correctness, completeness, and contextual relevance for the cleaning task.

Alignment involves mapping a knowledge graph assertion to an external source. This is typically followed by an interpretation step that extracts or evaluates the relevant information for use in detection or correction.
