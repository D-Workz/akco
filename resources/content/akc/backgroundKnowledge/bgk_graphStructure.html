Graph structure

A knowledge graph represents knowledge as a set of entities (nodes) and relations (edges) connecting them, collectively forming its graph structure. This structure serves as an internal source of background knowledge, and is generally independent of specific assertion types or their formal semantics.

The utility of graph structure depends heavily on the overall quality of the graph—specifically its correctness and completeness.

Graph structure is used in a variety of techniques. For example: it serves as training data in embedding-based models, as input for unsupervised learning in path-based approaches, and as a basis for statistical analysis.
