Human expertise

Human expertise represents a versatile form of external background knowledge. It can support a wide range of tasks including the design, configuration, and supervision of complex cleaning techniques.

Human input is particularly valuable for resolving semantic errors that require contextual or domain-specific interpretation. In general, there are two categories of contributors: anonymous crowds and domain experts (see crowdsourcing-based techniques).

Most approaches integrate human expertise with other methods, resulting in semi-automatic cleaning pipelines (see ontology-based, path-based, and hybrid approaches).
