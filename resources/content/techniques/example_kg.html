The example knowledge graph represents a geographical domain, defining a small ontology and assertions, shown in Figure 1. It contains several errors, including:

  • The wrong type and locatedIn property of sydney
  • A missing end-of-statement character in Line 4
  • A wrong literal type of the property population of australia
  • A literal value instead of an IRI instance in the property officialCurrency of houston
  • The population of beijing is the same as that of china

These errors are targeted and cleaned throughout the analysis.
